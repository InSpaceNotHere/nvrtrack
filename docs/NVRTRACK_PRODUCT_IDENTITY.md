# NVRTRACK product identity

This is the approved direction for a later design pass. This milestone does not restyle the application.

## Name

NVRTRACK

## Positioning

Business Improvement Command Center

## Tagline

Turn Ideas Into Progress

## Relationship

NVR Labs is the research and evidence engine. NVRTRACK is the business command center.

## Promise

Open one application, understand what needs attention, move approved improvements forward, and see whether they worked.

## Journey

Identify → Research → Plan → Implement → Measure

## Audience

Small-business owners, operators, consultants, service businesses, and eventually growing organizations.

## Visual direction for the final design phase

- Dark forest and jade navigation
- Warm ivory canvas
- Graphite text
- Soft semantic colors
- Premium rounded surfaces
- Calm motion
- Clear hierarchy
- Large readable labels
- Owner-friendly terminology
- Simple on desktop and on a phone

The previously approved visual mockup remains the future reference. The current milestone keeps the existing usable interface and adds the planning, implementation, and measurement loop.
